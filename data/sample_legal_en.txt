Legal Agreement - English Version

TERMS AND CONDITIONS

1. PARTIES TO THE AGREEMENT
This Legal Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of execution between:
Party A: The Company ("Company"), and
Party B: The Client ("Client").

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Company agrees to provide the following services:
- Consultation and advisory services
- Document preparation and review
- Legal representation as required
- Ongoing support and maintenance

3. PAYMENT TERMS
The Client agrees to pay the Company according to the following terms:
- Initial deposit of 30% upon signing
- Remaining 70% upon completion of services
- All payments due within 30 days of invoice
- Late payment fees: 1.5% per month on outstanding balance

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding:
- Proprietary information shared during the engagement
- Client data and personal information
- Business strategies and plans
- All communications and documents exchanged

5. TERMINATION CLAUSE
Either party may terminate this agreement with:
- 30 days written notice
- Outstanding payments must be settled immediately
- Return of all materials within 7 days
